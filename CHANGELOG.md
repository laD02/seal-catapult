# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
- New shop page on a bright bay painting: a tab list on the left, cards in the middle and the chosen item large on an
  ice podium on the right. Upgrades moved into its first tab.
- Skins in 7 kinds, bought with stars like the upgrades: fur colours, outfits, catapult balls, flight trails, impact
  effects, catapults and ropes. Skins show while you play a level.
- Two hand-drawn outfits, the Pirate Captain and the Ninja, each with its own drawing for every move the pup makes in
  a level.
- Eight hand-drawn catapults (Bamboo, Candy, Pirate Ship, Ice Crystal, Rocket, Volcano, Dragon, Royal) and eight
  hand-drawn ropes (jungle vine, candy cane, ribbon, hazard cable, ice, rainbow, gold chain, magic).
- The level map can be dragged on screens that crop it (tablets and long phones), so no level is ever out of reach; it
  opens on your next level, and a tap always picks the level nearest your finger.
- The upgrade shop now tells you exactly what the next level does, with the real numbers.
- Fixed: Blazing Pup could melt fewer ice blocks than Ice Spikes alone.
- Fixed: a tap on the hint text did not fire or dive; the pause menu's Map button could switch the music instead.
- The win and loss cards, Settings and How to play now fit long and tablet screens; small text is larger.
- The game now tells you when it could not save your progress, and tries again.
- The boss mission card says what it takes to win: beat the boss.
- Less battery use on the map, the pause menu and the result cards.
- The upgrade shop now has the painted home scene behind it; the About page has its animated cast.
- Pups knocked flying in the attack phase now tumble through a drawn somersault with a dust trail.
- Every character animation was redrawn at 16 frames for smoother motion.
- A pup knocked flying now tumbles in six styles and changes style at every bounce, with smoke puffing off it.
- The attack view is closer and the pup is bigger, so the action is easier to read.
- Smoother bears: every bear animation now has 16 frames instead of 4.
- A red close button on every pop-up; three new funny bear antics.
- New Seal Catapult logo, a proper loading screen with a progress bar, and a new loading animation between screens.
- Redesigned pause, win, loss, settings, stats and how-to-play screens with new funny animations; a language picker in Settings.
- New ice-crystal look for every button, panel and icon; buttons now sink and bounce when pressed.
- New home screen: a snowball battle between the seal camp and the bears' ice fortress, with seals and bears throwing
  and getting hit by snowballs.

- Ten castles, each with its own hand-painted map, one winding road and its own colours, from Frost Bay through the violet Glacier Canyon, the Aurora Forest, the sunset harbour, crystal caves, a volcano, blossom isles, a moonlit ice palace and the amber tundra to the Bear King's Throne. The catapult pit and the bear arena take on each castle's colours. Each castle has 41 levels.
- Every castle now looks different in play too: its own rock, ice, sky, far scenery and bridge in the catapult pit, and its own backdrop and stone in the bear arena (crystal canyon, pine forest, harbour, glowing caves, volcano, blossom garden, ice palace, amber tundra and the Bear King's storm).
- Every castle's map has its own weather: snowfall on the bay, embers over the lava, drifting blossom petals, leaves on the tundra wind, the aurora over the forest, glittering crystals and a lightning storm at the Bear King's Throne.
- On the map, your pup is bigger and easy to spot and waddles along the trail to it; caged pups jump and wave for help, with a big "HELP!" by the nearest cage; polar bears stand about by the trail and the castle boss shadow-boxes at the gate.
- The level map comes alive: fish leap out of the water, a freshly won level bursts into its star with twinkles, the next stone lights up before your pup hops to it, and the caged pup rattles its bars while it calls for help.
- After a win, the map shows your pup hopping to the next level before it starts, like the classic. Knocked pups now tumble every which way and roll along the ground curled up.
- Pups no longer curl into a ball when they hit something: they go flying like rag dolls, cartwheeling, back-flipping or corkscrewing, and every pup has its own way to fly out of the catapult, get stuck in the rock and lie dazed. All these animations are drawn with twice as many frames for smoother motion.
- A pup that crashes into the rock now lies flat on its back kicking, its helmet tumbling away, and crashed pups line up side by side instead of piling up; a diving pup now points head first along its dive.
- Ice now shatters like the classic: the block crumbles into a heap of ice chunks that hangs for a moment and twinkles away, chunks pop up, gold rings and smoke flash at the hit, and a rolling pup smashes straight through a whole row.
- Closer to the classic again: a struck pup flies out head first instead of spinning, with a flash where the snowball hits; the rope bends as it swings; a pup that hits a bear leaves its helmet on the bear's head; after bouncing, pups somersault and belly-slide down slopes.
- Castles without end, each a map of 60 levels like the classic: caged pups to free every 10 levels, two fish bonus runs and the castle boss at the end. Each castle is harder than the last. A new hand-painted map of three ice islands.
- Livelier, closer to the classic: a dive stays a head-first torpedo until it hits; a landed pup sits dazed where it fell; the swinging pup whips out wide; waiting pups cheer and look around; snow rings, rock flashes and floating ice bits on every hit; bears turn to watch a pup fly past; the boss tumbles out when beaten; the win card dances on a Perfect and the bears laugh when you lose; the map hero hops to the next level. Redrawn pups face you: waiting pups wave, tip their helmets, look around, hop, dance, yawn and sneeze, with more room on the bridge; the swinging pup grips the rope with both flippers and kicks on a fast swing; the mission card shows the pup putting its helmet back on.
